# CrewCover identity

CrewCover is an event operations tool. Its original mark combines a C-shaped coverage boundary with a confirmation check. It is drawn as simple SVG geometry, with no copied logo or stock artwork.

- `public/logo-mark.svg`: navy ground and mint mark for light contexts.
- `public/logo-mark-dark.svg`: mint ground and navy mark for dark contexts.
- `public/favicon.svg`: compact app mark.
- `public/og.svg` and `public/og.png`: original social image, exported at 1200 × 630 by `scripts/export-brand.mjs`.
- Typography: locally packaged Inter Variable, distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- App surfaces: navy structural chrome and light working areas. Green means covered, amber means proposed/pending, and coral means uncovered or cancelled. Text and icons carry the same states without relying on color.

The mark is original project artwork; no trademark-clearance claim is made. Reference research supplied interaction and information-hierarchy ideas, not copied component code. No paid assets or services were purchased.

Visual-asset license texts are preserved in `public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` (Tabler Icons MIT and Inter SIL Open Font License).
