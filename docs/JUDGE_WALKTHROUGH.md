This is a fictional conference and fictional volunteer roster. All coverage figures, proposals, assignments and audit entries come from real application operations.

## Reset before recording

Sign in to CrewCover before recording so Get started can open your event immediately. Open the demo conference if this is a new account. Choose **Reset demo**, then **Reset conference**. This resets only your synthetic event to 100% coverage. It does not delete accounts or change other organizers' events. Keep provider keys, settings and terminal windows out of the recording.

## Two to three minute recording script

1. **0:00–0:20** — Start on the public introduction, then choose Get started and open the event overview. “CrewCover helps an organizer recover staffing after last-minute cancellations. This fictional conference has twenty volunteers and twenty staffed positions.” Show full coverage.
2. **0:20–0:50** — In the morning shift, cancel Ada Okafor at Welcome desk, Noah Patel at Safety & access, and Mia Chen at Stage & technical. Confirm each cancellation. Show actual coverage falling to 85% and the three uncovered positions.
3. **0:50–1:30** — Open the embedded agent and submit: “Three volunteers have cancelled. Find qualified replacements without double-booking anyone. Read my current event and generate a repair proposal. Do not approve or apply it.” Wait for real tool execution. Explain that the agent reads persisted data and calls the same scheduling operations as the interface.
4. **1:30–2:00** — Open Repair Center. Inspect the actual recommendations and qualification, availability, overlap and hour-limit reasoning. “These are proposals; no assignment has changed yet.” Approve using **Review & approve**, then **Approve & apply repair**.
5. **2:00–2:20** — Show recovered coverage and the recorded approval in Activity. Refresh to demonstrate persistence.
6. **2:20–2:50** — Cancel Leo Mensah’s morning Guest assistance assignment, open Schedule, search for Sofia Park in the crew tray, drag her grip to that uncovered position, and choose **Approve assignment** after reviewing the checks. This is a vacancy fill, not a transfer of existing work. On mobile, choose the uncovered position and assign Sofia with the manual picker. Ask: “I manually changed an assignment. Recheck the current persisted coverage with get-event-coverage and report the current schedule revision.” Show that it reads the updated schedule.

If a candidate is ineligible or inference is unavailable, show the real error honestly. The deterministic Repair Center can generate proposals without inference. Do not describe a failed provider request as an agent demonstration.
